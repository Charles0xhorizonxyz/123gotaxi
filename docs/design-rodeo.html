All four directions use the Sarı Rodeo palette: taxi yellow, asphalt black, seat-cover cream and the checker strip. Each one gets a horse-rodeo logo and its own mood. The two-tap flow and screens are the same as before.

My pick

A by day, B by night, horseshoe in the browser tab

Daylight Bronco and Night Rodeo are one design system in light and dark, and the site switches automatically with the phone's setting. The bucking bronco is the main logo, and the buckle is the badge on taxi cards and stickers.

The horseshoe pin is the small icon (browser tab, home-screen shortcut), because it's the only logo that stays readable at 16 px.

Keep the Bucking Cab as a sticker or an Easter egg. It's too busy to be the main logo but too funny to waste.
